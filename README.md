Project Overview / Description:

An interactive web-based simulator designed to demonstrate Google Ads second-price auction mechanics. The application models how search engine result pages (SERPs) determine ad placements and costs based on advertiser competition, bidding strategies, and quality scores.

How to Interact with the Dashboard?


Access the Live Simulator:

Download & Open the "Ad Rank and CPC Dashboard. html" file in your web browser.


Adjust Auction Parameters:

Use the Ad Rank Threshold input field to set the minimum score required for an ad to be eligible for listing.


Manage Businesses:

Click the "+ Add Business" button to introduce new competitors into the auction.

Click the pencil icon (✏️) next to any business name to rename it, or click Remove to delete a participant.


Modify Bidding Inputs:

Adjust the Max Bid (₹) and Quality Score (0–10) for each business to see how performance variables shift in real time.


Analyze Results & Insights:

View the Auction Results Summary table, which instantly sorts winners by position and calculates their actual Cost Per Click (CPC).

Review the dynamic bar chart and Auction Highlights & Insights card to see who won 1st position, why they won, and what the average CPC is across the auction.
